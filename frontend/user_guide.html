01What Is JudiQ AI

JudiQ AI is a specialised legal intelligence platform designed for lawyers, litigators, and legal professionals practicing in Indian courts. It automates case analysis, legal drafting, and document intelligence under the Negotiable Instruments Act, SARFAESI Act, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, CPC, and CrPC.

Primary Jurisdiction
Courts of India — Magistrate, District, High Court, and Supreme Court
Core Technology
Gemini AI (Google) with deterministic legal rule engine fallback
Supported Statutes
NI Act 1881, SARFAESI Act 2002, BNS 2023, CPC 1908, CrPC / BNSS
Supported Languages
English (all tiers), Marathi and Hindi (Standard Plan and above)
Access Mode
Web application — no installation required

04How to Use JudiQ AI

Recommended Workflow — Document Upload Route (Fastest)

  1. Register or Sign In Create an account or log in with your existing credentials. Free Tier is activated automatically upon registration.
  2. Upload Case Documents Click "Upload & Extract Docs" from the dashboard. Upload cheque images, return memos, demand notices, or any relevant PDF. The AI extracts all facts automatically and creates a case docket.
  3. Review Extracted Facts Verify the extracted party names, amounts, dates, and statutory defects in the Document Intelligence panel. Correct any inaccuracies using the Manual Case Entry form.
  4. Run Case Analysis Select the appropriate engine (e.g., Section 138 for cheque bounce). The system generates a Case Readiness Score, strength indicators, risk flags, and applicable precedents.
  5. Generate Legal Drafts Open the Legal Draft Studio. Select the draft type (Legal Notice, Application 143A, Written Statement, etc.) and language. Download as PDF or Word document.
  6. Use Case Chat Open Case Chat for the loaded matter. Ask specific legal questions in the context of your case facts. The assistant uses your case data as its reference.

Alternative Workflow — Manual Case Entry

If documents are unavailable, click "Manual Case Entry" from the dashboard and complete the step-by-step intake wizard. All fields correspond to standard court document requirements. Once submitted, the system runs the analysis engine automatically.

06Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use JudiQ AI without uploading any documents?
Yes. The Manual Case Entry wizard accepts all inputs without requiring document uploads. However, document upload is faster and reduces the risk of data entry errors.
Are the legal drafts ready for court submission?
Drafts are formatted to standard Indian court conventions. It is recommended that a qualified advocate reviews and signs all documents prior to filing.
How is my case data stored?
Case data is stored in an encrypted PostgreSQL database. Each account's data is isolated and accessible only to the authenticated user.
What happens when my monthly report quota is exhausted?
You will be prompted to either purchase a Rs. 149 single report top-up or upgrade to the next plan tier. No existing case data is deleted.
Is Marathi or Hindi drafting available on the Free Tier?
No. Multilingual drafting (Marathi and Hindi) requires the Standard Monthly Plan (Rs. 999 per month) or higher.
Which court formats are supported?
Drafts follow Bombay High Court and subordinate District Court formatting conventions for Marathi matters, and standard national court formatting for English and Hindi drafts.
